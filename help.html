Getting started

A considered approach to caring for your car.

This guide walks you through the main areas of Cavallino Logbook. Use it as a quick reference while you organize your garage, record work and preserve the story of each vehicle.

01

Your Garage

Start by adding the vehicles you want to keep records for.

  1. Open the Garage tab.
  2. Select + Add a Car.
  3. Enter the vehicle details requested in the form, such as its make, model, year and mileage.
  4. Save the vehicle. Select its card whenever you want to work with that car's records.
Tip: Use consistent vehicle details and update the mileage whenever you record new work.
02

Maintenance Schedule

Keep upcoming and completed maintenance in view.

  1. Open Schedule.
  2. Review the maintenance items and their status.
  3. Add or update maintenance information using the controls provided in the app.
  4. After work is completed, record the service in the Service Log as well, so the history remains documented.
Maintenance guidance is a planning aid. Always follow the correct factory service information for your exact model and vehicle specification.
03

Service Log

Build a clear record of the work carried out on your vehicle.

  1. Open Service Log.
  2. Add a record for each service, repair or inspection.
  3. Enter the date, mileage, work performed, provider and cost where available.
  4. Use the notes area to capture useful details, including parts or follow-up recommendations.
Keep invoices and supporting documents. A log entry is most useful when it can be matched to original evidence.
04

Provenance

Preserve the ownership story and supporting details.

  1. Open Provenance for the selected vehicle.
  2. Review the provenance fields and complete the information you can substantiate.
  3. Keep supporting records such as ownership documents, historic invoices and other relevant evidence organized with your vehicle records.
  4. Use the provenance report feature when you want to assemble the information into a presentation-ready record.
Distinguish documented facts from owner notes or unverified history. Never present an unverified claim as authenticated provenance.
05

Ferrari Specialist Knowledge Center

Research model information, maintenance topics and known areas to investigate.

  1. Open Specialist Center.
  2. Browse the model and topic information available.
  3. Use it as a starting point when preparing questions for a qualified Ferrari specialist.
  4. Confirm technical specifications, service intervals, campaigns and repair procedures against authoritative factory information for your exact vehicle.
The Knowledge Center is an informational reference, not a substitute for factory workshop manuals, diagnostics or professional inspection.
06

Pre-Purchase Inspection

Bring structure to the evaluation of a car you are considering buying.

  1. Open Pre-Purchase.
  2. Start an inspection record for the vehicle being evaluated.
  3. Work through the available inspection categories and checklist items.
  4. Capture observations and add photo evidence where the app provides that option.
  5. Review open concerns with an independent marque specialist before making a purchase decision.
A digital checklist helps organize observations; it does not certify a vehicle's condition or replace a hands-on inspection.
07

Reference Register

Keep useful vehicle documents and reference information together.

  1. Open Reference Register.
  2. Add the reference details requested by the app.
  3. Use clear titles and notes so you can identify each item later.
  4. Keep original documents in a secure location and use the register to help track them.
08

Workshop Reports

Prepare a structured summary of vehicle work and findings.

  1. Open Workshop Report.
  2. Choose or confirm the vehicle details.
  3. Complete the available report fields with clear, factual information.
  4. Review the report carefully before using the print or export option provided in the app.
Check dates, mileage, costs and spelling before sharing a report with a buyer, workshop or insurer.
09

Your records & privacy

Understand where your logbook information is kept.

  • Device storage: Vehicle and logbook information is currently stored locally in the browser on the device you use.
  • Backups: Use the app's backup/export tools where available and keep a copy somewhere secure. Periodically verify that your backup can be restored.
  • Device changes: Clearing browser data, changing browsers or moving to another device can affect access to locally stored records. Export a backup before making changes.
  • Subscriptions: Pro billing requires the separate Netlify and Stripe setup to be completed. Do not assume paid access is active until checkout and subscription verification have been tested.

Keep every detail. Preserve every mile.

Your logbook is only as valuable as the records you maintain. Make entries promptly, keep original evidence and review your vehicle information regularly.
