# Ferrari Model Knowledge — Research Register

This register is a work queue, not a technical reference. No service interval, defect, repair method, torque value, fluid, part number or price should be published from this file.

## Evidence rules

Every proposed entry must include:
- Exact model, model year(s), market, variant and VIN/build applicability where known.
- System/component and a concise factual statement.
- Evidence classification: Ferrari factory publication; manufacturer campaign; government safety recall; independent specialist guidance; owner observation.
- Document/bulletin title and number, publication/revision date, source URL or controlled document reference, and date checked.
- Confidence and unresolved applicability questions.
- Reviewer name/organization for safety-critical or procedural content.

Prefer Ferrari TechInfo and the applicable owner/workshop documentation for factory specifications. Use NHTSA for U.S. safety recalls, investigations and complaints; these are not a substitute for Ferrari's own campaign lookup or a vehicle's repair history. Do not reproduce copyrighted factory manual pages or proprietary procedures without permission.

## Official research starting points

- Ferrari North America TechInfo: https://techinfo.ferrari.com/
- Ferrari VIN campaign lookup: https://techinfo.ferrari.com/recall
- NHTSA recalls: https://www.nhtsa.gov/recalls
- NHTSA datasets and API documentation: https://www.nhtsa.gov/nhtsa-datasets-and-apis

NHTSA warns its API is not intended for bulk VIN lookups. Keep any future recall integration user-initiated, rate-limited and transparent about coverage.
