# Ferrari Model Knowledge — Research Register

This register is a work queue, not a technical reference. No service interval, defect, repair method, torque value, fluid, part number or price should be published from this file.

## Evidence rules

Every proposed entry must include:
- Exact model, model year(s), market, variant and VIN/build applicability where known.
- System/component and a concise factual statement.
- Evidence classification: Ferrari factory publication; manufacturer campaign; government safety recall; independent specialist guidance; owner observation.
- Document/bulletin title and number, publication/revision date, source URL or controlled document reference, and date checked.
- Confidence and unresolved applicability questions.
- Reviewer name/organization for safety-critical or procedural content.

Prefer Ferrari TechInfo and the applicable owner/workshop documentation for factory specifications. Ferrari's TechInfo materials describe VIN/model-based access to workshop procedures, wiring diagrams, technical bulletins, campaign information, maintenance and parts information; subscription/access conditions apply. Ferrari also provides a VIN campaign lookup. Use NHTSA for U.S. safety recalls, investigations and complaints; NHTSA explicitly notes that its VIN lookup does not show completed recalls, some recent recalls, recalls older than 15 years, some specialty-manufacturer recalls, manufacturer service campaigns or international recalls. Neither database alone establishes a vehicle's complete repair history. Do not reproduce copyrighted factory manual pages or proprietary procedures without permission.

## Pre-purchase inspection workflow

The app now includes a general PPI recording template with identity/provenance, body/structure, cabin/electrical, mechanical/diagnostics, road-test and decision sections. Each item defaults to **Not inspected** and supports a condition rating, findings and an evidence reference. Saved reports can be printed and backed up locally. This is not a factory checklist, valuation, certification or substitute for an independent Ferrari specialist. The evidence field records a filename/reference only; photo uploads are not yet supported.

## Current research notes in the app

The starter entries across the 308, 328, 348, F355, 360, F430, 458, 488 and Roma are inspection prompts, not factory service instructions:
- F355: reported valve-guide/oil-consumption concerns; exhaust-manifold/catalyst inspection; timing-belt history and scope verification. These are based on secondary buyer-guide sources and require individual specialist assessment.
- 328/348: documented belt-service history, body/chassis condition, gearbox behavior and electrical/climate-control inspection.
- 360: historical Ferrari North America Technical Information Bulletin 1232 / Service Campaign 97 screening clue; clutch/F1-system history and diagnostics; suspension ball-joint condition. The bulletin copy's engine/VIN range is not a substitute for current Ferrari VIN/campaign verification.
- F430: exhaust-system condition and F1 hydraulic/clutch diagnostics.
- 458: VIN/campaign verification, DCT diagnostic scan/hot road test and interior control condition.
- 488: turbo-system assessment, DCT diagnostics, brake/tire condition and campaign history.
- Roma: battery/electrical function checks and VIN confirmation for NHTSA recall 22V-536.
